Why people switch from Willow, Wispr Flow and Superwhisper

Words as you speak

A small tab docked to the screen edge opens into a bar that shows the transcript live while you hold the key. Let go and it lands at the cursor.

100% on-device

Speech-to-text runs with WhisperKit on the Apple Neural Engine. Works offline. Audio never leaves the machine.

Any hotkey chord

Left and right modifiers are different keys. Default is left ⌃ + left ⌥, which collides with nothing. Hold to talk or press to toggle.

Pastes anywhere

Slack, Gmail, Xcode, Terminal, Notes, the browser. Wherever the cursor is. Your clipboard is restored afterwards.

Native and tiny

One Swift binary, a couple of megabytes. No Electron, no Tauri, no background helpers. Lives in the menu bar; the Dock icon is optional.

Polish on your Mac

On macOS 26, Apple Intelligence removes fillers, fixes punctuation and spells names from your dictionary, on-device, no key. Prefer Claude? Add your own key. Spoken commands and text replacements always work.

Undo, lock, retry

Undo sits on the bar for three seconds after a paste. Tap Space while holding to go hands-free. Try again re-runs a failed recording.

Takes the minutes

Record both sides of a Zoom, Meet or Teams call, no bot and no virtual driver. Transcript with speakers, your notes expanded, decisions and action items, one markdown file. Nothing uploaded.

Scriptable

speakhold://toggle or Speakhold --toggle drives the running app from SketchyBar, Raycast or a Shortcut.

Open source

MIT licensed. Read every line that touches your microphone. Issues and pull requests welcome.

The bar

Idle, it is a lozenge on the right edge of the screen. Hold the chord and it grows into a bar with a live waveform and the words so far; the newest words stay in view. Dots while it finishes, amber while Claude polishes, a tick when it has pasted. Position and the idle tab are settings.

Meetings

Choose Record meeting, or let Speakhold offer when it notices an app on a call. Your microphone and what the Mac is playing are recorded as two streams, so you and the other side are labelled without a bot joining the meeting. Type your own notes while it runs; tap the chord once to mark a moment; ask for the last two minutes if you drifted. Stop, and it transcribes on the Neural Engine, tells the far-side voices apart, names the meeting from your calendar, and writes Summary, Decisions, Action items and Open questions into one markdown file, with the full transcript underneath. The audio is deleted. Notes use Apple Intelligence on-device, or Claude with your key for long meetings.

How it works

Speakhold captures your microphone only while the chord is held, converts the audio to 16 kHz mono, runs it through Whisper Large v3 Turbo in-process, and types the result into the frontmost app with a synthetic ⌘V. The model (about 630 MB) downloads once on first launch. Polish runs on-device through Apple Intelligence. The only network request the app can make is the Claude engine, and only if you choose it and provide a key. Meetings add a Core Audio process tap for what the Mac is playing, behind macOS's "System Audio Recording Only" permission; the audio is transcribed locally and deleted.

Built on WhisperKit. Made in Wellington, New Zealand by Wild Interactive.
